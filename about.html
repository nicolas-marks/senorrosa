About the project

Meet Señor Rosa

Señor Rosa is my little corner of the internet,a place for stories, projects, adventures, and lifelong learning.

This website is my digital playground—a place where curiosity, technology, creativity, and real life come together. It's equal parts portfolio, journal, comic strip, and laboratory for new ideas.

Who I Am

I'm an IT engineer, Army veteran, gamer, gardener, traveler, musician, and lifelong student. I'm endlessly fascinated by what happens when curiosity meets technology. Whether I'm building software, learning Spanish, exploring Mexico, or creating comics with AI, I'm happiest when I'm learning something new.

What I’m Building

Señor Rosa is becoming the intersection of my work and my hobbies. It's where AI experiments become real projects, everyday moments become comics, travel becomes storytelling, and lifelong learning becomes something worth sharing. As the journey continues, this site will grow into both my creative portfolio and the future home of my consulting and travel business.
