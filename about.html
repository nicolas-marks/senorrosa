About Señor Rosa

I build things to learn how they work.

Señor Rosa is my little corner of the internet: part journal, part comic strip, part portfolio, and part laboratory for ideas that sounded interesting enough to try.

I work professionally in technology, but most of what you see here started with curiosity: learning a new tool, solving a real problem, telling a true story, or seeing whether AI could help turn an idea into something useful.

03 · Small Business Project

8A Pro Services

Lead generation · Public data · AI training · Web support

A local small-business owner told me their biggest challenge wasn't technology. It was finding leads. I started there.

I built a practical workflow around public procurement and contract-award data to identify larger contractors that could be worth approaching for subcontracting work. AI helped turn large tables of public information into a smaller, actionable list that a business owner could actually work from.

The project expanded into a simple training website and hands-on guidance for using AI, managing their web presence, and generating additional leads independently.
