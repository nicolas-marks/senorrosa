One evening in Mérida, Gina, Leyslie, Jake, and I walked from our casa through the barrio to pick up a Little Caesars pizza.

On our way home, a police truck pulled beside us. The officer leaned out and said:

“Buenas noches, ese es un perro enorme.”

Gina and Leyslie stayed silent, waiting for my baby Spanish to come out.

“Buenas noches, claro que sí.”

The officer continued, amused by Jake’s boots:

“Tu perro trae zapatos. Nunca había visto un perro con zapatos. ¡Qué chistoso!”

My brain buffered.

“Zapatos rojos.”

The officer smiled, waved, and drove away. We all started laughing while Jake side-eyed the pizza box.

A warm little Yucatán moment.
