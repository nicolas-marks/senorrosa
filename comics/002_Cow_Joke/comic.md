# Cow Joke

Driving through the Oregon countryside with Gina, we passed a field full of cows.

Like any mature adult, I immediately announced:

"Did you know starting in 2027 they can't make round hay bales anymore?"

She sighed.

"Are you serious? Why?"

I pointed toward the cows.

"Well... the cows were complaining about not getting..."

I paused for dramatic effect.

"...three square meals a day."

She buried her face in her hands.

"I hate you."

Totally worth it.
