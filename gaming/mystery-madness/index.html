How the mode works

Release rules: 5v5 Open Queue with a 1–2–2 role composition, best-of-3 Control. Your assigned role offers three hero choices. Levels earn choices between two random powers on death or round transition; no fixed power capacity is documented here.

Death grants 10% Ultimate Charge. Respawning grants 40% maximum-life Overhealth and 30% movement speed while out of combat. These are baseline mechanics, not selectable powers. Current hotfix verification is incomplete.
