Workshop resources

Prompt patterns

  • Goal + context + constraints + output format
  • “Ask me the questions you need before you begin.”
  • “Give me three approaches and explain the tradeoffs.”
  • “What assumptions are you making?”
  • “What should I verify before I act on this?”

Three things to remember

  • Give AI context. Better source material usually produces better work.
  • Understand your files. Know what they are, where they live and how to move them.
  • Protect your business. Work from copies, protect sensitive information and verify important output.
